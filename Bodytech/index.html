Scenario multiplier scales computed benefits: Conservative 0.6×, Base 1.0×, Aggressive 1.4×. Staff redeployment savings are included in all scenarios.

Adoption ramp reflects that not all 600K members will be on AI from Day 1. Year-1 benefit is weighted by the month-3/6/12 adoption assumptions above — adjust the sliders to reflect your ramp plan.

Retention uplift is calibrated against Amigo's own modeling: +5pp renewal rate on a 600K base at $360/yr ≈ $10.8M/yr. At 8pp with full adoption, the math yields ~$17.3M — consistent with Amigo's cited $18M figure.

Collection rate improvement is the most conservative estimate: 7pp on $18M/mo billing = $1.26M/mo in recovered revenue. AI past-due recovery is additive.

NPV is calculated at a 12% annual discount rate (≈ 0.95%/mo), matching the discount rate used in Amigo's Bodytech deck. Setup cost is treated as the Year-0 outflow.

All figures are estimates for planning and discussion purposes only. Results will vary based on actual volumes, billing rates, system integration depth, and adoption. COP conversion uses the exchange rate set in Global Business Assumptions (default: 4,200).
